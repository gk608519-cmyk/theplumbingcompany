## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-craft digital footprint for an established American mechanical contractor specializing in both complex commercial infrastructure and high-end residential systems. The tone balances raw industrial competence with executive-tier refinement—conveying structural permanence, meticulous tradecraft, and responsive reliability. 

The aesthetic synthesizes **Modern Industrial Precision** and **Architectural Minimalism**. Layouts avoid decorative flourishes in favor of structured geometry, calibrated micro-textures, engineered borders with metallic finishes, and purposeful white space. Interfaces should evoke the tactile precision of machined brass fittings, heavy gauge piping, and blueprint clarity, framed within an executive digital experience optimized for rapid, trust-driven customer conversions.

## Colors

The palette establishes heavy physical authority through deep wine-red vitrified clay and oxidized iron tones, balanced by clean architectural neutrals and precision metallic sheens:

- **Primary Burgundy (`#741C27`)**: The principal brand voice. Used for prominent structural actions, high-impact CTA containers, status highlights, and foundational anchor elements.
- **Deep Maroon (`#4A1019`)**: The grounding weight. Employed for high-emphasis states, hover transitions on primary surfaces, rich dark headers, and contrast-critical architectural panels.
- **Metallic Silver (`#B8B9BC`)**: The mechanical link. Leveraged for engineered dividers, precision borders, technical badge accents, and hair-line edge highlights.
- **Charcoal Black (`#171719`)**: Grounded neutral. Replaces generic pure black to offer an ironwork density for headline typography, deep footer framing, and structural chassis blocks.
- **Clean White (`#FFFFFF`) & Light Grey (`#F4F4F5`)**: Baseline surface canvas tiers. Used to isolate technical data, guarantee uncompromising contrast, and evoke spotless operational hygiene.

## Typography

The type system pairs structural, geometric authority with clear, high-legibility body prose:

- **Headlines (Montserrat)**: Geometric, confident, and unapologetic. Rendered in upper weights with slight negative tracking on large scales to evoke industrial cast lettering, architectural plans, and structural steel stamps. All caps should be used selectively on `label-sm` and badge headers to enforce job-site clarity.
- **Body & Data (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly balanced, neutral, and readable across all viewport sizes. The soft humanist curves counter the rigid structural headlines, reducing cognitive load when users scan technical scopes, bids, or diagnostic service estimates.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Montserrat)**: Designed with strict uppercase styling and tracked-out letterspacing for quick status determination, license validations, emergency markers, and operational metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an authoritative 12-column architectural grid on desktop, collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablets and a focused 4-column structure on mobile devices. 

- **Grid Dynamics**: Structural containers rely on a maximum content constraint of `1320px` to maintain optimal scanning paths and line lengths for service breakdowns and bid requests. 
- **Rhythm**: Vertical space follows an 8px base rhythm. Dense data sections (such as service specs, parts lists, and line-item estimates) use compact vertical spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`), whereas trust-focused marketing spreads and case studies use spacious section padding (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` via section margins).
- **Responsive Adaptations**: On mobile breakpoints, side margins compress to `1.25rem` to prioritize content density, and callout components pivot to full-width edge-to-edge frames with subtle metallic boundary lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material cues rather than exaggerated digital drops. Depth is communicated through engineered layers, metallic borders, and tinted low-diffusion groundings:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base surfaces sit on `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F4F5`. Elevated components (cards, dialogs, technical drawers) deploy crisp 1px borders using `#B8B9BC` with an opacity between 40% and 70%, evoking machined aluminum and brushed steel flanges.
- **Layered Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are cool-tinted and multi-stop:
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 2px 4px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.04), 0 8px 16px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.06)`.
  - *Active / Hover Lift*: `0 4px 8px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.06), 0 16px 32px rgba(116, 28, 39, 0.08)`. The subtle burgundy tint on the secondary shadow bloom hints at brand weight under direct light.
  - *Modals & Emergency Drawers*: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(23, 23, 25, 0.25)` framed with a distinct 1px outline in `#B8B9BC`.
- **Inverted Mechanical Surfaces**: High-urgency modules (e.g., 24/7 Rapid Dispatch or Emergency Gas/Water Shutoff CTAs) utilize a solid `#171719` Charcoal chassis with hairline Burgundy borders and subtle inner edge-bevels.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (Level 1)** corner geometry (`0.25rem` / `4px` default radius). 

This decision reinforces structural solidity and industrial equipment ergonomics—avoiding juvenile high-radius curves while remaining refined compared to brutalist 0px edges:
- Base components (inputs, standard buttons, tabular data rows): `4px` (`0.25rem`).
- Cards, modules, and dispatch panels (`rounded-lg`): `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- High-level containers and hero chassis (`rounded-xl`): `12px` (`0.75rem`).
- Pills are strictly reserved for live status indicators (e.g., "ON DUTY", "DISPATCHED", "LICENSED & BONDED").

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (Emergency/Conversion)**: Solid `#741C27` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `label-lg` uppercase tracking. Finished with a subtle 1px internal border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`) and a `4px` corner radius. Hover state shifts instantly to Deep Maroon (`#4A1019`) with a slight upward translate (`-1px`).
- **Secondary Action (Commercial Inquiries/Specs)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp 1.5px `#171719` border, transitioning to `#171719` background with white text on hover.
- **Utility / Tertiary**: Text-only button accompanied by an architectural directional arrow icon (`→`), featuring an underline that expands on hover.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height: 48px to allow easy, error-free tapping on mobile job sites.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` encased in a 1px `#B8B9BC` border.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#741C27` with a non-blurry `0 0 0 1px #741C27` mechanical ring.
- Labels: Placed above fields in `label-md` uppercase typography with a `#171719` fill.

### Cards & Service Chassis
- Enclosed in an exterior 1px border (`#B8B9BC` at 50% opacity) on a `#FFFFFF` surface.
- Top card edges for premium commercial services feature an optional 3px structural accent strip in Primary Burgundy (`#741C27`).
- Interior content follows strict padding hierarchy (`space-lg`).

### Badges & Chips
- Status Badges: Compact uppercase text set in `label-sm`. Backgrounds use desaturated tints (e.g., `#F4F4F5` with a `#B8B9BC` border and `#171719` text). 
- Emergency Badge: Solid `#741C27` fill with white text and a gentle pulsating beacon indicator.

### Lists & Technical Specifications
- Spec grids rely on alternating `#FFFFFF` and `#F4F4F5` rows separated by hairline `#B8B9BC` borders (40% opacity).
- Unordered lists use solid square markers in `#741C27` rather than standard round bullets.

### Conversion & Emergency Bar
- Fixed or top-pinned chassis for immediate emergency dispatch calls. Rendered in `#171719` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` typography, a live mechanical indicator light, and a primary phone CTA button in `#741C27`.
